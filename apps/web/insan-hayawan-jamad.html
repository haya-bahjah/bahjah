One letter. Five columns. Sixty seconds.

The game everyone already knows from a sheet of paper. A letter is called and everybody races to fill every column with it. An answer nobody else wrote is worth ten; one somebody matched is worth five each — so the win goes to whoever thinks of what the room did not.

3–8 players· Phones + one big screen· Arabic & English
