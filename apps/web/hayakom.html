Party game — Hayakom

Welcome to a Saudi home. Come in, make yourselves at home.

A guided tour of a Saudi house, played on the big screen with your phones. Walk from the Majlis to the kitchen, the library and the courtyard, answer a question about Saudi life in every room, and race your guests to the final challenge.

1–12 players · best on a TV or laptop, with everyone answering on their phones · played in English

How a visit works

Open the door, tour the house, take the crown.

The house

Every room has a story.

The Majlis always opens the visit, then four more rooms follow, picked by the host or drawn at random. Each one opens a different side of Saudi life.
