Game 3 of 3 — Knows You Best

How well do you know your circle?

A wholesome game about friendship and empathy, not deception or speed. Answer personal prompts, then guess who wrote what — the player who understands the group best wins.

How a round works

Answer, guess, connect.

For example

One prompt, four answers, one guess.

"What is your favorite color?"

Everyone answers privately, then the answers are shuffled — can you match each one to the right person?

Emma →Green
James →Purple
Olivia →Blue
Noah →Black
Question categories

Pick your category, set the tone.

The host picks one or more categories in the lobby — mix them for a night that starts light and goes deep.
