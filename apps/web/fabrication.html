Everyone lies. One of you is telling the truth.

You get a strange but true question — without the answer. Invent one that sounds real, then find the real one hiding among everybody else's lies. Points for being right, points for being believed.

3–10 players Phones + one big screen Arabic & English
Bubble wrap was originally invented and sold as what?
APacking for eggs
BWallpaper
CA swimming float
DGreenhouse insulation

Three of these were written by your friends.

How a round goes

01

Read the fact

A real, verifiable, slightly ridiculous question goes up on the big screen. Nobody sees the answer.

02

Write your lie

On your phone, invent an answer convincing enough that the room picks it instead of the truth.

03

Find the truth

Every lie and the real answer go up together, shuffled. Pick the one you believe — you cannot pick your own.

04

The reveal

The truth is stamped, every lie is traced back to whoever wrote it, and you find out exactly who you fooled.

Start a room

The host runs the big screen. Everyone else plays on their phone.
