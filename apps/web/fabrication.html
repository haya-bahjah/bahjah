How to play
Weird but true

Everyone lies. One of you is telling the truth.

Get a strange fact with no answer. Write a fake good enough to fool your friends. Then find the real one.

3–10 players 15–25 min Phones + one big screen
How it works
1
Read the fact.A weird-but-true question lands on the big screen. No answer.
2
Write your lie.Make it believable. +2 for every player it fools.
3
Find the truth.Every lie plus the real answer, shuffled. +2 if you pick right.

The host runs the big screen and doesn't play. Everyone else plays on their phone.
