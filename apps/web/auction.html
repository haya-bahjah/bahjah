How many can you name? Prove it.

A category goes up for auction. Bid how many answers you can name; the room raises you or passes. Win the lot and the clock starts: name every one, or the bid is worth nothing.

How a lot goes

I can do that. I can do more. Sold.

Scoring

All or nothing.

The higher the bid, the bigger the swing. Make it and you score exactly what you promised; fall one short and the lot is worth nothing.
