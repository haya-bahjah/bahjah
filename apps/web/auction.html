How a lot goes

01

A lot is called

A category goes up on the big screen — colours, capitals, cartoon characters.

02

Bid your nerve

Each player in turn raises the number or passes. Pass and you are out of this lot for good.

03

Sold

When everyone else has passed, the lot belongs to the highest bidder — and so does the problem.

04

Deliver, or get nothing

Name them all against the clock. Make the bid and it scores exactly what you promised; fall one short and it scores zero.

Start a room

The host runs the saleroom and rules on the answers. Everyone else bids from their phone.
