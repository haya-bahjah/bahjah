# Verdict

The game-over screen, in two worlds: the Mafia's red-black night or the Citizens' sunrise.

- **Mafia won** — radial `verdict-mafia-glow → verdict-mafia-ground → black`, drifting smoke, a breathing red bloom, a heavy vignette. Headline in `bone` with a red halo; kicker *MAFIA WON THE GAME* in `mafia-red` between two fading rules. Card fan: the boss card upright, two dimmed hitman cards tilted ±15°. Play again is `mn-btn--hot`.
- **Citizens won** — sky radial `verdict-dawn-sky → mid → low → night-navy` with sunburst rays; headline in `verdict-dawn-ink` with a white halo.
- Below: winner pills (token + name + role), then three stats (Rounds, Mafia/Saved, Survivors) in `stat` type split by hairline rules.
- On phones the title and actions are pinned and the middle pages, so *Play again* is always on screen.
