# FinalWinner

The game-over screen: the question *Who knows you best?* answered by one name. It's the only screen that shows a score, and only the winner's.

- The title asks the question (Arabic: *مين عارفكم؟*). The winner's photo or character sits in a 5px frame in their accent with a glow, rotated -1.4°, under a crown.
- The name is set at 30–56px, weight 800, in the winner's colour. Below it, the **final tag** sticker has the winner's colour fill, a 3px ink outline and a solid `shadow-ink` offset.
- The phone adds the player's own score (62px) and share targets: 48px hand-cornered squares that glow cyan on hover and green once copied.
- Share copy: *Who knows you best? Latifa 🏆 Play Knows You Best on Bahjah*.
