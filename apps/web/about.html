About

Why we built Bahjah.

Bahjah started with a simple idea: the best nights happen when people put their phones down together, not apart. We build multiplayer party games that work equally well in Arabic and English, so no one has to sit out the joke, the vote, or the round.

What we believe

Built for real rooms, not just feeds.

Bilingual by default

Every game, every screen, and every round works fully in Arabic or English — never a bolted-on translation.

For every age

From family living rooms to game nights with friends, our games are built to be genuinely fun for the whole table.

Fair pricing

Try everything free for 6 hours before you ever add a payment method — you'll know exactly what you're paying for.
