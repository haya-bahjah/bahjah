About

Why we built Bahjah.

Bahjah is a bilingual — Arabic and English — group party games platform, built to bring more fun and connection into gatherings with family and friends. Whether you're meeting at home, celebrating a special occasion, or just looking for a different way to break the routine, Bahjah gives you an easy, enjoyable experience that makes everyone part of the game.

We took our name from the feeling we want every gathering to have: bahjah — joy. That's why we build a variety of games that combine challenge, wit, quick thinking, and laughter, through content that stays close to our culture and fits every kind of gathering.

Our games need no prior experience or complicated setup — just gather the people you love, pick a game, and get ready for a night full of energy and moments you'll remember for a long time.

Our ambition

We aim to create entertainment experiences that bring people together, strengthen the connection between them, and turn everyday gatherings into memories full of joy.
